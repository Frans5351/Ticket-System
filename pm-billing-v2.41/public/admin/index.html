Park Manor Admin

The trustees' tracker and billing

Add this page to your home screen to get the trustees' app as its own icon, separate from the residents' report page.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
Open the trustees' app

The first time you open the new icon you will need to log in once.
